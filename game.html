    What changes the population?

    Births need paid care, habitat health of at least 50%, and spare habitat capacity. Isolation reduces births and increases mortality. Crowding, poor health, unpaid bills, and threats cause losses. Vaccination reduces yellow fever losses; fire teams reduce fire losses. Bridges connect groves, but trees create habitat. See Budget details for the most recent week.

    All rates, costs, and population targets are fictional game settings, not field predictions.
