    Survival guide

    Spend slowly. Earn trust. Keep reserves.

    1. Fund the people first

      Outreach raises community trust; monitoring creates grant evidence. Both draw cash each week. Turn programs on or off as your runway changes.

    2. Choose a network

      Seven routes connect six groves. Each agreement adds an ongoing obligation. One crew builds at a time; complete a bridge before planting along that route.

    3. Care for what you built

      Canopy development takes four years of funded care. Drought affects seedlings; storms damage bridges. A bridge below 45% condition closes. Trees below 60% survival do not count as a mature corridor.

    4. Prove recovery lasts

      After Year 8, meet every target for three consecutive annual reports, including stable funds. A bad year resets that streak.

    Running out of money

    At zero, projects stop and bills go unpaid. An emergency appeal is slow and small; it cannot replace a funding plan. Twenty-six consecutive unpaid weeks ends the campaign.

    Other ways to lose

    Ten consecutive years of falling closing balances ends the program, even with cash left. Losing all local tamarin families also ends the campaign.

    The model

    This fictional scenario compresses ecology into playable rules. Forest Credits and wildlife indices are game values. Bridge-first planting is the game’s progression system.
