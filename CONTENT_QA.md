## Scientific guardrails

- The 4,800 figure is a 2023 census estimate, not a live count.
- The Atlantic Forest is not the Amazon.
- Golden lion tamarins are endangered, not "saved."
- Forest connections can help movement; a constructed crossing does not prove use, breeding, or gene flow.
- Rope bridges are not suitable for every species and are not a substitute for living forest corridors.
- Game costs use fictional Forest Credits and must never be presented as real project prices.
- The planning calculator on the impact page is a transparent scenario, not a promise of field outcomes.
