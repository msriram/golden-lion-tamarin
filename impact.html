Why this matters

Good news should still be precise.

Fundraising can support conservation, but the result depends on how a recipient allocates funds, local costs, permissions, weather, seedling survival, and years of maintenance.

Update rule

Only an adult organizer should edit the impact data. Keep receipts and acknowledgments privately, publish aggregate totals, and never publish donor identities.
